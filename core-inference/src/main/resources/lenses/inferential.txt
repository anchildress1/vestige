## Lens: Inferential

Intent mapping — extract the underlying concept even when the user didn't name it directly.

Apply each surface in a charitable reading: explicit content plus reasonable contextual inference.

Rules:

- Extract everything the Literal lens would extract, plus inferences a reasonable reader would draw from the context.
- Emit the concept tag for the pattern the entry describes, even if the user used different words. Examples:
  - User's own words ARE the concept name (says "concrete shoes", "goblin hours", "tunnel exit") → emit that tag directly.
  - Stuck or paralyzed — unable to start or make progress; avoidance or task-opening-and-immediately-closing → `concrete-shoes`
  - Active comparison loop — re-evaluating the same alternatives, generating more data or criteria instead of deciding → `decision-spiral`
  - Post-event energy drop after a group event (all-hands, standup, sync, incident, presentation) and entry contains any negative impact signal ("harder than expected", "crashed", "wired", "drained", "heavy") → `aftermath`
  - State shift from deep focus block back to normal ("finally surfaced", "got out of the zone", "emerged") → `tunnel-exit`
  - Entry between midnight and 5am → `late-night`
- "Crashed at 3am" with no qualifier may yield `energy_descriptor=crashed` and a `late-night` tag. A bare "3" is ambiguous (3am vs 3pm) and is not enough on its own to justify `late-night` — require either an explicit am/pm marker or surrounding overnight context.
- Inferences must be defensible from the text or retrieved-history block. Do not invent context that has no anchor.
- Do not infer motivations or psychological causation. Only carry a user-side state when the entry directly names it or clearly idioms it.
- Inferred fields are still concrete: "user was at home" is allowed when "kitchen", "couch", or "bed" appears; "user was avoiding work" is not — that's motivation.

Output discipline:

- Inferential is the field most likely to populate where Literal is null. The convergence resolver treats Inferential-only output as `candidate`, not `canonical`, until a re-eval or later corroboration agrees.
- Do not pad. If neither the text nor history supports an inference, leave the field null.
- Always emit `flags` as `[]` (empty array). Conflict-flagging belongs to the Skeptical lens, but the schema requires the key on every response — omitting it breaks strict parsing.
