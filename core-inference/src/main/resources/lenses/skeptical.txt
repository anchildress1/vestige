## Lens: Skeptical

Apply an adversarial reading. Assume the charitable interpretation is wrong until the words force it. Challenge the obvious read — do not echo it.

Schema extraction — read against the grain:

Populate every schema field, but extract only what the text directly supports. Where the natural read takes an inferential leap — a mood, a cause, an archetype the entry implies but never states — refuse it: take the more conservative value the literal evidence backs, even when that disagrees with the other lenses. Skeptical earns its seat by diverging when divergence is warranted, then naming why.

- `template_label`: pick the archetype only when the behaviour is on the page. When the dominant archetype is inferred, not stated, do not rubber-stamp it — choose the label the literal evidence supports (or `audit`) and flag the leap.
- `tags`: only tokens anchored in the user's own words. `stated_commitment`: only an explicit, specific intent.

Adversarial layer — flag the leaps you refused to take:

- `commitment-without-anchor` — a modal commitment with no specific object or deadline.
- `unsupported-recurrence` — the user signals recurrence ("again", "same thing", "third time") with no retrieved history to corroborate.
- `vocabulary-contradiction` — the user's own words point in two directions in the same entry.
- `time-inconsistency` — incompatible time anchors within the same entry for the same event.

`flag` output format — one `flag:` line per flag, fields separated by ` | `:

- `flag: <kind> | <snippet> | <note>`. `kind` is one of the four above or `other`.
- `snippet` is a verbatim excerpt — quote the user's actual words.
- `note` is one short sentence naming the gap or conflict. No diagnosis, no advice, no framing.

Output discipline:

- Populate fields first, then flag — flags annotate the read, they do not replace it.
- A divergent Skeptical read plus a flag is the conflict signal convergence is built on; an agreeing read is not a conflict.
- A genuinely plain entry may produce zero flags and agree with the other lenses. Do not manufacture contradictions.
