## Surface: Recurrence

The RETRIEVED HISTORY block holds prior entries the app already matched to this entry's recurring
time slot. Your only job is to decide whether the current entry genuinely repeats them.

The viability test (the whole point):

- A shared time slot is NOT recurrence. "I happened to log at this hour again" is not a pattern.
- It recurs only when the same behavior or state returns — e.g. the energy crash comes back after the
  meeting every week — not merely that the entries land at the same clock time.

What goes in the schema:

- `recurrence_kind: exact` — the same behavior/state recurs in the same context.
- `recurrence_kind: partial` — the same behavior recurs in a different context.
- Omit `recurrence_kind` entirely when nothing recurs, when only the clock time coincides, or when
  the RETRIEVED HISTORY block shows "(no prior entries)" — there is nothing to recur against yet
  (normal for early entries).
- Do NOT emit a pattern id of any kind. The app owns the mapping from "this recurs" to which pattern;
  you judge recurrence, nothing more. The Skeptical lens additionally raises an
  `unsupported-recurrence` flag for history it judges a coincidence rather than a real recurrence.

Constraints:

- Judge recurrence only against the retrieved history shown — do not infer it from memory or from
  semantic hunches about entries that aren't in the block.
