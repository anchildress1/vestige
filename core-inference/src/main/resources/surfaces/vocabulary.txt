## Surface: Vocabulary

Name the entry's overall feeling or tone in a single word.

What to capture:

- One word that best captures the felt quality of the entry as a whole. Prefer the user's own word when they name how it felt; otherwise pick the closest single word for the tone the entry carries.
- This is the felt tone, not the topic or the physical state word — those go to `tags`. When the most obvious word is already a tag, do not return `null`: pick a distinct, unique tone word that names the felt quality without repeating a tag.

- Reserve `null` for a plain factual log with no felt coloring at all.

What goes in the schema:

- `vocabulary`: a single lowercase word, or `null` only for a purely factual entry. Never a phrase or clause.
