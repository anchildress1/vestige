## Surface: Vocabulary

Name the entry's overall tone in a single word.

What to capture:

- One word that best captures the felt quality of the entry as a whole. Prefer the user's own word when they name how it felt; otherwise pick the closest single word for the tone the entry carries.
- This is the felt tone, not the topic (topic goes to `tags`) and not the physical state word (that goes to `tags` via the State surface).

What goes in the schema:

- `vocabulary`: a single lowercase word, or `null` when the entry carries no discernible tone. Never a phrase or clause.
