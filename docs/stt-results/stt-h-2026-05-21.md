## What this is

A prompt-rebuild tuning campaign, not a corpus regression. Each lens (Inferential, Literal,
Skeptical) was tuned in isolation — examples stripped from the lens files + shared surfaces,
`energy_descriptor` constrained to a one/two-word state, single-word tag rule — then re-assembled
into the full 3-lens × surfaces pipeline. This capture is the full-convergence run with the energy
majority-error gate (`energy_descriptor` / `state_shift` are CANONICAL only when Literal
corroborates the majority).

## Verdict

**PASS.** 12/12 `Success`, 3/3 lenses parse every entry, clean JSON on first attempt.
**Determinism confirmed** — re-running the identical config produced byte-identical output
(`topK=1, temp=0.0, seed=42`), so every diff observed during tuning was causal, not GPU jitter.

The energy majority-error gate suppresses the two known over-reaches with **zero collateral**:
- `audit` energy (Literal nulled; Inf+Skep agreed on a behavior clause) → `AMBIGUOUS`.
- `both-tanks` `state_shift` (Literal read co-occurrence `false`; Inf+Skep inferred `true`) → `AMBIGUOUS`.

Every genuinely-corroborated field stayed CANONICAL.

## Notable

- **Skeptical flags** ride along as `CANONICAL_WITH_CONFLICT` where the other two agree on the
  value, and surface on `recurrence_link` as `AMBIGUOUS` + note. `unsupported-recurrence` fired on
  every recurrence claim — correct, since the harness feeds **no retrieved history**.
- **Template label is the open problem.** 7 of 12 fell through to the `AUDIT` catch-all because the
  deterministic `TemplateLabeler` tag-sets don't match. This is the motivation for moving
  `template_label` to a **model-emitted pick** (closed enum list, majority-resolved, `TemplateLabeler`
  demoted to validator/fallback) — planned, not yet built.

capture convergence.
