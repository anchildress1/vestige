## What this is

Observation-only capture of the rebuilt pipeline: flat-line lens output (no JSON), the five surfaces
(behavioral / state / vocabulary / commitment / recurrence), and `template_label` now **model-emitted
per lens and majority-resolved** rather than picked by the deterministic `TemplateLabeler`. No
assertions — the harness logs one `TUNING` line per entry. This is the planned label change from the
2026-05-21 notes, now live.

## Verdict

**PASS (parse + convergence).** 12/12 `Success`, 3/3 lenses parse every entry, single attempt, zero
retries, zero parse failures. The flat-line contract holds across all 36 calls.

**Two standing problems, unchanged by the label move:**
- **AUDIT catch-all still dominates** — 8/12 resolve to `AUDIT`. Model-emitting the label did not
  beat it; the model itself reaches for `audit` on under-specified entries the same way the
  `TemplateLabeler` tag-sets did. Non-AUDIT only where the entry is unambiguous (TUNNEL_EXIT,
  AFTERMATH ×2, DECISION_SPIRAL).
- **Vocabulary mode-collapse** — 10/12 resolve to `weary` (all Inferential-sourced). The vocabulary
  surface is barely varying; the drift the demo's vocab-frequency beat depends on is not there on
  this corpus.

## Notable

- **Per-lens template split is visible and resolves sanely.** `doc-done-one-sitting`: Literal=audit,
  Inferential=tunnel-exit, Skeptical=tunnel-exit → **TUNNEL_EXIT**. `migration-rewrite-loop`:
  Literal+Inferential=decision-spiral, Skeptical=audit → **DECISION_SPIRAL**. Majority resolution is
  doing its job; the problem is the *base rate* of `audit`, not the resolver.
- **The conflict beat fires** — `both-tanks-empty` produced a real CONSENSUS_WITH_CONFLICT on tags
  from a Skeptical `vocabulary-contradiction`. That's the Skeptical-red moment the demo wants.
- **Skeptical `commitment-without-anchor` over-reaches twice.** On `crashed-3pm-no-warning` it flagged
  "Had to give up on the rest of the afternoon" as a deadline-less commitment, and on `wired-third-night`
  it flagged a description of anxiety as one. Neither is a commitment. The flag is firing on
  non-commitment clauses — worth tightening, but it self-suppresses (commitment stays AMBIGUOUS, not
  stored).
- **`recurrence_kind` never fabricated** despite no history — every lens left it null. The
  no-history → no-recurrence behaviour is clean on this corpus.
