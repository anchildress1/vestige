## What this is

Three changes, all aimed at making the model pick correctly at the root rather than a deterministic
map deciding after the fact:

1. **Model commits to the template.** `output-schema.txt` reworded off "pick `audit` when no archetype
   dominates" to "commit to the one archetype that best fits". The model's converged pick is
   authoritative — no `TemplateLabeler` tag→archetype map overriding it (that map is deleted).
2. **Goblin Hours is deterministic.** Rule: the model's pick stands; the *only* override is — when the
   model picked nothing specific (`audit`, or didn't converge) **and** the entry's local capture hour
   is in the midnight–5am window, the label becomes GOBLIN_HOURS. A model `goblin-hours` pick stands on
   its own; the override only ever *promotes* `audit`, never demotes a real pick. `TemplateLabeler` is
   now a single `isGoblinHours(capturedAt)` clock predicate.
3. **Per-lens vocabulary sampler** (unchanged from prior commit): `VOCAB_DIVERSITY_SAMPLER` on the
   Inferential lens only; the other two stay greedy.

## Verdict

**PASS.** 12/12 `Success`, 3/3 lenses parse, zero retries. Every final `template_label` below is the
model's own converged pick (or, for the one goblin entry, the model's converged goblin pick).

- **AUDIT dropped 8/12 → 4/12.** The four that remain are genuine: all three lenses say `audit` on
  `audit-double-check` and `both-tanks-empty`; `wired-third-night` and `anxious` are majority `audit`.
- **Six distinct archetypes** in play (AFTERMATH ×3, TUNNEL_EXIT ×2, AUDIT ×4, GOBLIN_HOURS,
  DECISION_SPIRAL, STALLED) — up from near-total audit.
- **Vocabulary held:** `weary` 10/12 → 6/12, distinct tone words 3 → 7 (`weird`, `restless`, `sudden`,
  `wired`, `anxious`, `contradictory`).

## Goblin Hours — what this run does and does NOT prove

- `awake-2am-spinning` resolved **GOBLIN_HOURS**, but on this run that is the **model's own** converged
  pick (lens votes: `goblin-hours / goblin-hours / audit`), not the deterministic override firing.
- The deterministic override (`audit` + capture-hour ∈ 00:00–04:59 → GOBLIN_HOURS) is **not exercised
  by this corpus**: the only goblin-window capture (`awake-2am`, 02:13 local) is one the model already
  labelled goblin, and the four `audit` entries are all daytime captures (15:22 / 08:00 / 14:00 / 20:00
  local) — so they correctly stay AUDIT, which confirms the override does not false-fire. The override
  itself is proven by unit test (`BackgroundExtractionWorkerTest`), not by this run.
- This is the robustness the design buys: even if the model had punted `awake-2am` to `audit`, the
  02:13 timestamp would have rescued it to GOBLIN_HOURS. The text saying "2am" is irrelevant — the
  clock decides (cf. `both-tanks-empty`, whose text says "1am" but was captured at 20:00 → not goblin).

## Honest reads — the picks aren't all perfect

Committing has a cost: two picks are the model's genuine-but-debatable read.
- `crashed-3pm-no-warning` → **STALLED** (the model anchored on "had to give up on the afternoon"),
  where AFTERMATH ("Crashed at 3pm") is the more obvious read.
- `tuesday-stalled` → **AFTERMATH**, where STALLED ("completely unable to get moving") is arguably closer.

These are the model's own reads — the point of the change is that the model *picks*, across the full
archetype range, instead of hiding behind `audit`. Tightening borderline reads is prompt-quality
follow-up, not a deterministic override.

## Caveats

- **Latency ~38 s mean** vs 21 s baseline — this was a back-to-back GPU session; per-lens times roughly
  doubled across the board, reading as thermal throttling, not a cost of the change. Re-measure cold.
- **Single run**, seeded sampler — reproducible per prompt; determinism not separately re-verified.
- **Cross-branch baseline** — the 2026-05-23 baseline ran on a different branch; the archetype-commit
  and vocabulary deltas are visible directly in this run's per-lens data regardless.

## Follow-ups

- Borderline archetype reads (`crashed-3pm`, `tuesday-stalled`) — prompt-quality tuning, evidence-first,
  no per-archetype descriptions.
- ADR-002:135 lists Goblin Hours in the model's convergence enum; it needs an addendum on `main` to
  record that goblin is now a deterministic timestamp call.
- Confirm latency is thermal with a cold-device re-run.
